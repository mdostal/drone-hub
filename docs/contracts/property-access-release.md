## 1. Authority & Access
Owner represents that Owner **owns or is authorized to grant access** to the Property, and hereby
grants Provider permission to operate a small unmanned aircraft (drone) over and around the
Property and to capture aerial photographs, video, and sensor data on `[DATE / date range]`.

## 2. Grant of Rights (Footage Release)
Owner grants Provider a **perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license** to use, reproduce, edit, and
publicly display the captured media and any derived deliverables (including interactive tours and
property-intelligence views) for Provider's **portfolio, website, social media, and marketing**.

- ☐ **Owner opts OUT** of Provider's marketing/portfolio use. (If checked, media is used only for
  Owner's own deliverables and not published by Provider.)

## 3. No Identifiable Persons / Neighbors
Provider will make reasonable efforts to avoid capturing identifiable individuals and neighboring
private property in published media, and will **exclude or blur** any that appear. Owner will not
request capture of neighboring property without that owner's permission.

## 4. Privacy & Data
Provider will not publish the Property's street address, owner name, or other personal information
without separate written consent. Raw data is stored securely by Provider.

## 5. Release & Hold Harmless
Owner releases Provider from claims arising from lawful operation conducted with reasonable care,
except for gross negligence or willful misconduct. Provider maintains `[$1,000,000]` drone liability
insurance (COI on request).

## 6. Regulatory Note
Provider operates under FAA **Part 107** and obtains any required airspace authorizations. This
release does not waive any third-party or governmental restriction on flight.
