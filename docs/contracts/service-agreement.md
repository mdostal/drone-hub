## 4. Regulatory Compliance
Provider operates under **14 CFR Part 107** and holds a valid FAA Remote Pilot Certificate.
Provider is responsible for airspace authorizations (LAANC), Remote ID, and aircraft registration.
Client is responsible for ensuring Provider has lawful **access and authority** to fly over the
Property (see the Property Access & Footage Release).

## 5. License & Usage Rights
Upon full payment, Client receives a **non-exclusive, perpetual license** to use the delivered
media for marketing the Property. Provider **retains ownership** of all raw footage, source data,
and the underlying property-intelligence tooling/components. Provider retains the right to use
delivered and raw media in Provider's **portfolio and marketing** unless Client opts out in writing
(see Release). Property-intelligence reports are provided for informational/decision-support use.

## 6. Property-Intelligence Disclaimer
Any terrain, boundary, slope, drainage, acreage, or measurement information is **derived from public
GIS/LiDAR data and non-survey-grade aerial capture** (no RTK; typical positional accuracy ~1–3 m).
It is **not a legal survey, boundary determination, or engineering document** and must not be relied
upon as such. Consult a licensed surveyor/engineer for authoritative determinations.

reasons at no penalty. Client cancellations within `[24 hours]` of a scheduled flight may incur a

## 8. Insurance

Provider's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid. Provider is not liable
for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Nothing limits liability for gross negligence
or willful misconduct where non-waivable by law.

## 10. Indemnification
Client indemnifies Provider against claims arising from Client's lack of authority to permit flight
over the Property or from Client's use of the deliverables.
